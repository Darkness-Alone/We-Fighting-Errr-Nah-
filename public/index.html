    Sources & Method

      Index comes from the news wire. Every headline from the last 48 hours gets a smoke level 1–5 by keyword (nuclear > invasion > strikes > military > tension). Newer headlines count more, and talk of ceasefires and peace deals counts half.

      TheseHandsCon steps up every 20 index points (5 below 20, 1 at 80+), and tightens one extra step if nuclear chatter passes 25 (about three nuclear headlines).

      Beef Clock is this site's own conversion of the index into minutes to midnight. The official Doomsday Clock is set once a year by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists.

      For entertainment and situational awareness. Real DEFCON levels are classified; nothing here is one.
